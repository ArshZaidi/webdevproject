How it works

Three steps to a reserved table

  1. 01

    Browse & choose

    Filter the menu by category, search by name, and open any dish for the full detail.

  2. 02

    Build your booking

    Add dishes to the cart, adjust quantities, then pick a date and a time slot.

  3. 03

    Track it live

    Your booking appears instantly in the kitchen dashboard. Watch the status change as it's cooked.

Why Aurum

Built for people who plan their meals

No queues, no guesswork. Reserve what you want, when you want it, and let the kitchen do the rest.

  • Real-time status

    Pending, Accepted, Preparing, Ready, Completed — updated by the kitchen, visible to you.

  • Honest availability

    Sold-out dishes are flagged immediately and can't be booked by mistake.

  • Cancel with control

    Change your mind before the kitchen starts cooking. After that, your slot is locked in.

  • Simple pricing

    What you see on the card is what you pay. Totals update live as you adjust quantities.
